# 🔍 Problem Statement: The Jamtara SIM-Swap Epidemic

### The Real-World Crisis
In FY2023, highly organized cybercrime rings based out of regions like Jamtara (Jharkhand) executed over 95,000+ coordinated UPI fraud cases. These syndicates leverage identity theft and SIM-swapping to compromise banking applications, moving stolen assets across multiple layers of transient money mule accounts before converting them into untraceable cash or cryptocurrency.

### The Investigating Bottleneck
Field investigators within State Police Cyber Cells face an overwhelming influx of evidence data. Currently, analysts manually cross-reference disconnected, flat spreadsheets containing bank transaction ledgers, telecom Call Detail Records (CDRs), and device registries (IMEI numbers). This manual correlation process takes weeks per network trace.

### The Impact of Delay
Because cyber cells lack specialized relationship network visualization tools, the vast majority of these cases remain unsolved. The manual triage lag gives criminal kingpins a massive window to dismantle their operations, rotate burner devices, discard compromised SIMs, and evade law enforcement completely.
